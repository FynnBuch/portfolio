Fluid Mechanics Project: Cargo Box

University project · Fluid Mechanics, TU Berlin

Overview

In this project we investigated how a roof-mounted cargo box changes the airflow around a car. We designed the cargo box, 3D printed it and assembled a 1:18 scale model. The airflow around the car was then measured in a wind tunnel, with and without the box. Finally, we compared the results with CFD simulations to validate them.

My contribution

  • Design of the cargo box
  • Preparation of the 3D-printed parts
  • Testing and simulating the airflow around the car body

Tools

  • SolidWorks – CAD modelling
  • Python and MATLAB – calculations
  • OpenFOAM – CFD simulations
  • LaTeX – scientific documentation

Key takeaways

I learned how to apply fluid mechanics theory in a design process, and where that theory reaches its practical limits. I gained experience testing a model in a wind tunnel, interpreting the results and scaling them up to the full-size vehicle, as well as running and validating CFD simulations.
